14-Day Itinerary

Every day is 100% flexible. These are just ideas. Sleep in, skip things, or wander. Green-bordered days are intentionally open.

Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo ✈️
  • ✈️ Land at Narita (NRT). Buy Suica/Pasmo IC card at the airport kiosk.
  • 🚄 Narita Express or Keisei Skyliner to Tokyo (~60–90 min, ~$25–$30).
  • 🏨 Check in, drop bags, freshen up.
  • 🍜 Casual dinner: gyudon, ramen, or conveyor sushi near the hotel.
  • 🛍️ 7-Eleven/FamilyMart run for snacks and drinks.
  • 😴 Rest up. No late plans — jet lag is real.
Day 2 – Shibuya & Harajuku
  • ⛩️ Meiji Jingu Shrine — forest path, calm, easy walk.
  • 🛍️ Harajuku: Takeshita Street (crepes, quirky fashion).
  • 🚶 Omotesando: tree-lined avenue, flagship stores.
  • 🌃 Shibuya Crossing — view from Mag's Park or Starbucks.
  • 🍦 Dessert: matcha soft serve or taiyaki.
  • 🍶 Izakaya dinner in Shibuya back-alley.
Day 3 – Akihabara & Ueno
  • 🤖 Akihabara: anime/manga/electronics (Animate, Yodobashi, Radio Kaikan).
  • 🎮 Multi-floor arcades (Sega, Taito, Club Sega).
  • ⛩️ Ueno Park: temples, museums, zoo.
  • 🛍️ Ameya Yokocho market street — snacks, pharmacy, clothes.
  • 🍣 Sushi conveyor belt or tempura dinner.
Day 4 – Asakusa & Odaiba
  • ⛩️ Senso-ji Temple — Tokyo's oldest. Nakamise shopping street.
  • 🚢 Sumida River walk or short cruise.
  • 🗼 Tokyo Skytree — views + Solamachi mall.
  • 🌇 Odaiba: waterfront, teamLab or Gundam statue.
  • 🍡 Dango or taiyaki from street vendors.
Day 5 – TeamLab & Ginza
  • 🎨 TeamLab Planets or Borderless (book online in advance).
  • 🛍️ Ginza: Uniqlo flagship, stationery, window-shopping.
  • 🌳 Hamarikyu Gardens (traditional garden with teahouse).
  • 🍱 Tsukiji Outer Market for fresh sushi breakfast/lunch.
Day 6 – Kamakura Day Trip
  • 🚃 JR Yokosuka Line to Kamakura (~1 hr, ~$5).
  • 🕉️ Great Buddha (Daibutsu) — open-air bronze statue.
  • ⛩️ Hasedera Temple — garden, hydrangeas in June.
  • 🥾 Short hike: Daibutsu trail to Hase (~20 min, gentle).
  • 🍤 Shirasu don (whitebait rice bowl) for lunch.
  • 🚃 Return to Tokyo by evening.
Day 7 – Open Day / Optional Hike

No plans required. Sleep in, wander, or pick an option below.

  • ⛰️ Mt. Takao: ~1 hr from Shinjuku. Moderate hike, temple, Fuji views on clear days.
  • 🏙️ Shinjuku deeper: Tokyo Metropolitan Gov (free views), Kabukicho neon walk, Omoide Yokocho alley.
  • 🛍️ Ikebukuro: Anime stores, Sunshine City.
  • 🛍️ Nakano: Nakano Broadway vintage manga/collectibles.
  • 🎬 Studio Ghibli Museum: Requires advance tickets (buy early).
Day 8 – Night Walks & Views
  • 🏙️ Shinjuku: Tokyo Metropolitan Gov Building (free sunset).
  • 🌃 Kabukicho — neon, Godzilla head, safe to stroll.
  • 🍜 Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane): yakitori stalls, beer, grilled skewers.
  • 🍸 Golden Gai: tiny bars, calm drink, observe nightlife.
  • 🍰 Dessert: Japanese cheesecake or matcha parfait.
Day 9 – Shopping & Last Tokyo
  • 🛍️ Morning: Akihabara last anime/tech buys (tax-free).
  • 🛍️ Afternoon: Shibuya/Shinjuku clothes, Don Quijote, souvenirs.
  • 🎮 Arcade session if energy remains.
  • 🍱 Farewell izakaya dinner in Tokyo.
Day 10 – Tokyo → Osaka
  • 🚄 Shinkansen Nozomi/Hikari to Shin-Osaka (~2.5 hr, ~$130, reserve seats).
  • 🏨 Check in to Osaka hotel (Namba or Umeda).
  • 🛍️ Quick neighborhood + convenience store run.
  • 🌃 Evening: Dotonbori — neon signs, takoyaki, okonomiyaki.
  • 🛍️ Shinsaibashi shopping arcade nearby.
Day 11 – Osaka Classics
  • 🏯 Osaka Castle — museum inside, great park.
  • 🍢 Shinsekai: retro neighborhood, kushikatsu (fried skewers).
  • 🐟 Kuromon Ichiba Market — sushi, tamagoyaki, fruit.
  • 🏙️ Umeda Sky Building floating garden observatory.
  • 🍮 Dessert: matcha pudding or taiyaki.
Day 12 – Anime Tech & Night Walk
  • 🤖 Den Den Town (Nipponbashi) — Osaka's anime/tech district.
  • 🛍️ Namba Parks or NMB48 area.
  • 🏮 Hozenji Yokocho — stone alley, lantern-lit calm night walk.
  • 🍧 Kakigori (shaved ice) or taiyaki late night.
  • 🛍️ Don Quijote Namba for cheap souvenirs and snacks.
Day 13 – Kyoto Day Trip
  • 🚄 Special Rapid Service to Kyoto (~30 min, ~$4).
  • ⛩️ Fushimi Inari Taisha — hike through torii gates (~1–2 hrs).
  • ⛩️ Kiyomizu-dera — wooden temple, valley views.
  • 🚶 Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka streets — traditional shops, tea.
  • 🍵 Matcha + sweet set in Higashiyama.
  • 🚄 Return to Osaka by evening.
Day 14 – Kobe/Himeji or Free / Departure

Flexible last day. Choose whatever sounds good.

  • 🏯 Option A – Himeji: ~45 min. White Heron Castle. Back to KIX.
  • 🐄 Option B – Kobe: ~30 min. Harborland, Kitano, Kobe beef lunch.
  • 🛍️ Option C – Osaka: Last-minute souvenirs at Shinsaibashi.
  • ✈️ Afternoon/evening transfer to Kansai Airport (KIX) via Haruka (~45 min).
  • 🛫 Fly KIX → SFO nonstop.

Essentials

🌤️ Weather & Clothing

June in Tokyo/Osaka: 70–85°F (21–29°C), humid, afternoon rain. Quick-dry layers, umbrella, and waterproof shoes are essential. Evenings can feel cool after rain.

🚄 Transport Cheat Sheet

  • Buy Suica/Pasmo at airport — tap everywhere.
  • Shinkansen: reserve seats, buy ticket at station.
  • Hyperdia or Jorudan for train times.
  • JR Pass is not worth it for this route.

📶 Connectivity

  • Pocket WiFi: ~$8/day, shared by 6 people.
  • eSIM: Ubigi, Airalo — no device swap.
  • Coverage is excellent in cities.

💬 Emergency Phrases

  • Sumimasen — Excuse me / Thank you
  • Arigato gozaimasu — Thank you
  • Eigo ga hanasemasu ka? — Do you speak English?
  • Toire wa doko desu ka? — Where is the bathroom?
  • Kore o kudasai — This, please

📲 App Recommendations

  • Google Maps — Transit, walking, restaurant hours.
  • Google Translate — Camera mode for menus.
  • Hyperdia / Jorudan — Exact train times.
  • Tabelog — Japanese restaurant reviews.

💴 Money Logistics

  • Cash is king at markets and small stalls.
  • 7-Eleven ATMs accept foreign cards.
  • Use Splitwise or Venmo to split hotel/food bills.
  • Many places are still cash-only.

👥 Group Coordination

  • Pick a landmark meeting spot if split up.
  • "Last person leaves" note on hotel door.
  • Agree on a station clock / big statue as fallback.
  • Share live location briefly if someone is late.

🙇 Etiquette

  • No tipping in Japan — it's polite to refuse.
  • Shoes off indoors (hostels, temples, ryokan).
  • Stand left on escalators (Tokyo) or right (Osaka).
  • Quiet on trains — no loud calls.
  • Temples: wash hands/mouth at temizuya, no central offering photos.

☔ Rainy-Day Backups

  • TeamLab (indoor digital art — book ahead).
  • Underground malls (Crystal City, Umeda).
  • Don Quijote (endless floors of stuff).
  • Arcades (Sega, Taito — fully indoor).
  • Covered shopping arcades (Shinsaibashi).
